# Advanced AI: NLP & Computer Vision

## 1. Natural Language Processing (NLP)

### Content Generation

**Models**: GPT-based models and transformers.  
**Use Cases**:

- Headline & Ad Copy generation.
- Email subject line and body content optimization.
- Personalization based on audience segments.

### Sentiment Analysis

**Social Listening**: Real-time tracking of brand mentions and crisis detection.  
**Optimization**: Sentiment-driven messaging and emotional targeting.

## 2. Computer Vision

### Visual Content Analysis

**Content Recognition**: Object detection, brand logo detection, and scene understanding.  
**Aesthetic Analysis**: Composition scoring (rule of thirds), color impact, and technical quality assessment.

### Visual Optimization

**Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO)**: Automatically optimizes image formats and performs visual A/B testing based on user preference models.
