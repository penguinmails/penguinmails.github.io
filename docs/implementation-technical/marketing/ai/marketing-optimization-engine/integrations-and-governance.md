# Integrations, Ethics & Infrastructure

## 1. Platform Integrations

### Ad Platforms

- **Google Ads**: Automated bid/budget/keyword optimization via API v14.
- **Facebook Ads**: Lookalike audience and placement optimization.
- **LinkedIn Ads**: B2B account-based targeting optimization.

### MarTech Stack

- **CRM**: Salesforce (Lead scoring) and HubSpot (Nurturing).
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics (Attribution) and PostHog (Funnel optimization).
- **Email**: SendGrid/Mailgun (Deliverability and send time optimization).

---

## 2. Ethical AI & Compliance

### Fairness & Bias

- **Detection**: Check for demographic parity and equalized odds.
- **Mitigation**: Data preprocessing and fairness constraints in algorithms.

### Transparency

- **Explainability**: SHAP feature importance and decision reasoning documentation.
- **Privacy**: Differential privacy and GDPR/CCPA compliance by design.

---

## 3. Infrastructure & Monitoring

### ML Infrastructure

- **Compute**: p3.2xlarge for training, c5.4xlarge for inference.
- **Storage**: S3 model registry and feature stores (Feast).
- **MLOps**: Kubernetes (Kubeflow) and MLflow for monitoring.

### Model Monitoring

- **Accuracy**: Continuous tracking of drift and precision/recall.
- **Business Impact**: ROI measurement and revenue attribution to AI.
- **Improved Cycle**: Automated hyperparameter tuning and model selection.
