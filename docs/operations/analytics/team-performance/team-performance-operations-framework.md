## Strategic Goals

- **Agile Velocity**: Driving consistent 15%+ improvements in sprint delivery capacity.
- **Quality Assurance**: Maintaining 80%+ test coverage and <1% production defect density.
- **Operational Scale**: Supporting enterprise-grade multi-tenant staff coordination and support workflows.
