# Testing Strategies & Best Practices

To get the most out of A/B testing, you should focus on variables that have the highest impact on your campaign's success.

## 1. Subject Line Strategies

The subject line determines if your email is even opened.

- **Tone**: Professional vs. Playful vs. Urgent.
- **Length**: Short (3-5 words) vs. Explanatory (7-10 words).
- **Personalization**: Using `{{firstName}}` or `{{company}}`.
- **Formatting**: Testing emojis or specific capitalizations.

## 2. Content & CTA Strategies

Once opened, the content must drive action.

- **Length**: Short, punchy copy vs. long-form educational content.
- **Call to Action**: "Learn More" vs. "Get Started" vs. "Reply to this email."
- **Visuals**: Text-only vs. including a logo or hero image.
- **Social Proof**: Including a testimonial vs. focusing on features.

## 3. Sender Name Optimization

The sender name builds trust before the user clicks.

- **Individual**: "Jane Doe"
- **Company**: "PenguinMails Team"
- **Combination**: "Jane from PenguinMails"

## 4. Best Practices

- **Test One Variable**: Don't test the subject line AND the content at the same time, or you won't know which change caused the lift.
- **Ignore Small Lifts**: If the difference is less than 1-2%, it might just be noise. Look for statistically significant changes.
- **Avoid Over-Testing**: Don't test every single email; focus on high-volume or high-value campaigns.
