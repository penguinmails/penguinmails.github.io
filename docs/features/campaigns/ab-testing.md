# A/B Testing

A/B testing is a foundational feature of PenguinMails that allows you to optimize every aspect of your outreach through controlled experiments. By testing variations on small segments of your audience, you can ensure that your full campaigns are as effective as possible.

## A/B Testing Modules

Explore the following modules for implementation guides and technical references:

### 1. [A/B Testing Quick Start](/docs/features/campaigns/ab-testing/quick-start)

Create your first split test, configure variants, and set your winning criteria.

### 2. [Testing Strategies & Best Practices](/docs/features/campaigns/ab-testing/testing-strategies)

Learn how to test subject lines, content, and sender names for maximum impact.

### 3. [Experiment Design & Statistical Significance](/docs/features/campaigns/ab-testing/experiment-design)

Understanding sample sizes, multi-variant testing, and data-driven confidence scores.

### 4. [Technical Specification](/docs/features/campaigns/ab-testing/technical-specification)

Developer reference for the randomization engine, database schema, and automated winner deployment.

---

## High-Level Capabilities

- **Automatic Winner Selection**: No manual intervention needed to deploy the winning content.
- **Statistical Rigor**: Integrated confidence scoring to eliminate guesswork.
- **Multi-Variant Support**: Test up to 5 variations simultaneously.
- **Cross-Metric Optimization**: Optimize for opens, clicks, or even replies.
