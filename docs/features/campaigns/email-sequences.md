# Email Sequences

Email Sequences enable you to design sophisticated, adaptive customer journeys that automatically respond to lead behavior. From simple welcome series to complex, multi-path sales funnels, sequences ensure that your outreach is consistent and timely.

## High-Level Capabilities

- **Intelligent Branching**: Create custom paths for engaged vs. unengaged users.
- **Goal-Based Automation**: Automatically stop outreach once a lead converts.
- **Dynamic Timing**: Send emails based on specific events or user-defined delays.
- **Modular blue-printing**: Save your best-performing workflows as templates for easy reuse.
