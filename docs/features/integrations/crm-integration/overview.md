## Strategic Goals

- **Alignment**: Bridging the gap between marketing outreach and sales follow-up.
- **Visibility**: Providing a 360-degree view of the customer journey within the CRM.
- **Automation**: Eliminating manual data entry through real-time bi-directional sync.

---

## Timeline (Q1 2026)

- **Phase 1**: Research & OAuth flow design.
- **Phase 2**: Salesforce implementation (Priority 1).
- **Phase 3**: HubSpot implementation (Priority 2).
- **Phase 4**: General Availability & Setup Guides.

---

## Success Criteria

- **Reliability**: 95%+ sync success rate across all connected tenants.
- **Latency**: Engagement data synced to CRM in <5 minutes.
- **Data Integrity**: Zero data loss during high-volume batch operations.
