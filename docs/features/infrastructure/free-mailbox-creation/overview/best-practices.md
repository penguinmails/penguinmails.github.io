# Best Practices & Account Management

## Best Practices for Email Accounts

### **Level 1: Essential Guidelines**

- **Professional Format**: Use business-appropriate naming conventions
- **Consistent Branding**: Maintain uniform email signatures
- **Security Basics**: Use strong passwords

### **Level 2: Professional Practices**

- **Naming Conventions**: Balance role-based (sales@) and name-based (john@) accounts
- **Profile Optimization**: Use professional headshots and complete bios
- **Reputation Building**: Implement gradual warm-ups and focus on content quality
- **Legal Compliance**: Ensure all communications meet regulatory requirements

### **Level 3: Enterprise Excellence**

- **Portfolio Reputation**: Manage reputation across hundreds of domains
- **Lifecycle Management**: Complete account lifecycle and data-driven optimization
- **Risk Assessment**: Regular assessment and implementation of governance frameworks

## Account Management

### **Level 1: Basic Management**

- **Account Overview**: View status and performance from the dashboard
- **Quick Actions**: Perform common tasks directly from the overview

### **Level 2: Professional Management**

- **Usage Statistics**: Track sending volumes and performance metrics
- **Maintenance Tasks**: Regular health checks, security audits, and settings optimization
- **Scaling Strategies**: Plan for growth and implement smart account rotation

### **Level 3: Enterprise Operations**

- **Multi-tenant Management**: Oversee accounts across multiple companies or clients
- **Strategic Planning**: Data-driven portfolio growth and competitive analysis
- **Process Automation**: Implement automated quality assurance and training programs
