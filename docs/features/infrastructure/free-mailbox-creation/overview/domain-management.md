# Domain Management & DNS

### **Level 1: Basic Domain Selection**

- **Professional Domains**: Choose business-appropriate domains
- **Brand Alignment**: Match domains to your company name

### **Level 2: Strategic Management**

#### Domain Selection Strategy

- **Industry Relevance**: Match domains to your business sector
- **Memorability**: Select easy-to-remember domain names
- **Professional Appearance**: Avoid suspicious or spammy-sounding domains

#### DNS Configuration

- **SPF Records**: Automatically configure SPF for email authentication
- **DKIM Setup**: DomainKeys Identified Mail for email integrity
- **DMARC Policy**: Domain-based Message Authentication policies
- **MX Records**: Mail exchanger records for proper email routing

#### SSL Certificate Management

- **Automatic SSL**: Free SSL certificates for secure email transmission
- **Certificate Renewal**: Automatic renewal and health monitoring
- **Compliance**: Ensure compliance with international email security standards

### **Level 3: Enterprise Management**

- **Multi-domain Portfolios**: Sophisticated management across large portfolios
- **Domain Age Optimization**: Leverage domain age for improved deliverability
- **Geographic Distribution**: Global domain distribution strategies
- **Advanced DNS**: Custom records, disaster recovery, and failover management
- **Threat Intelligence**: Integration with threat detection systems
