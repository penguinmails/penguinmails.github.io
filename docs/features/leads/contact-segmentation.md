Contact Segmentation allows you to divide your contact database into meaningful groups based on demographics, behavioral patterns, and custom criteria. This ensures your campaigns are precisely targeted for maximum engagement and ROI.

## Segmentation Modules

Explore the following modules for detailed guides and technical specifications:

### 1. [Segmentation Quick Start](/docs/features/leads/contact-segmentation/quick-start)

Basic concepts, creating your first segment, and understanding the difference between static and dynamic lists.

### 2. [Advanced Segmentation Strategies](/docs/features/leads/contact-segmentation/advanced-strategies)

Deep dive into behavioral targeting, lead score integration, complex logic, and exclusion rules.

### 3. [Segmentation Analytics & A/B Testing](/docs/features/leads/contact-segmentation/analytics-testing)

How to measure segment performance and run A/B tests to validate your segmentation hypothesis.

### 4. [Technical Specification](/docs/features/leads/contact-segmentation/technical-specification)

Developer-focused documentation covering the database schema, rules engine architecture, and API endpoints.

## Strategic Goals

- **Precision Targeting**: Reaching the right audience with zero waste.
- **Improved Deliverability**: Higher engagement rates within segments improve sender reputation.
- **Automated Lifecycle Management**: Moving contacts between segments automatically as they interact with your brand.
