# Segmentation Analytics & A/B Testing

Measure the effectiveness of your segments and refine your targeting criteria through data-driven testing.

## Segment Performance Tracking

Each segment provides a performance dashboard to track its health and impact over time.

- **Key Metrics**: Average Open Rate, Click Rate, Unsubscribe Rate, and Revenue Generated.
- **Growth Trends**: Monitor segment size growth and engagement lift compared to the general contact database.

## Segment A/B Testing

Testing your segmentation hypothesis ensures you are targeting the most receptive audience.

### Testing Framework

1. **Hypothesis**: "Contacts with a Lead Score > 60 convert better than lower-scored engaged contacts."
2. **Setup**: Create two segments (A: Score > 60, B: Score 40-60).
3. **Execution**: Send the exact same campaign to both segments.
4. **Comparison**: Analyze Open, Click, and Conversion rates.
5. **Action**: Refine your scoring model or communication strategy based on the winning segment.

## Compliance & Health

- **Recalculation Monitoring**: Track how often dynamic segments update to ensure real-time targeting.
- **Unsubscribe Monitoring**: High unsubscribe rates in a specific segment may indicate over-targeting or poor message-market fit.
