# Advanced Segmentation Strategies

Go beyond basic filters with multi-layered logic and behavioral triggers to create hyper-targeted contact segments.

## Complex Rule Logic

### AND/OR Combinations

You can create nested groups of conditions to capture broad yet specific audiences.

- **Example**: Match contacts who are either "High-Engaged" OR have a "High-Value" lead score.
- **Exclusions**: Use "Match ALL" combined with "Tag does NOT include" to prune segments of unqualified leads.

## Behavioral Segmentation

### Email Engagement Patterns

- **Click-But-Never-Convert**: Target users who show high interest (10+ clicks) but haven't performed a conversion action.
- **Device Usage**: Segment for mobile-only users to optimize for mobile-first email designs.
- **Time-Based**: Target users based on their most common open times (e.g., "Monday Morning Readers").

### Campaign-Specific Behavior

Filter by interaction with specific campaigns or URLs. For example, contacts who clicked a "Pricing" link but did not click the "Book a Demo" button in your last product launch.

## Integration & Maintenance

### Lead Score Integration

Leverage the [Lead Scoring](/docs/features/leads/lead-scoring) system to create "Hot," "Warm," and "Cold" lead segments automatically based on their behavioral scores.

### Custom Field Segmentation

- **Multi-Select Categories**: Filter by lists of interests or product categories.
- **Date-Based Triggers**: Build segments for trial expirations or account anniversaries.
- **Numeric Ranges**: Segment by Lifetime Value (LTV) or revenue thresholds.

### Segment Exclusions & Suppression

- **Global Suppression**: Exclude "Unengaged" or "Recent Purchasers" from broad newsletters.
- **Fatigue Management**: Exclude contacts who have received any campaign within the last 7 days to avoid over-messaging.
