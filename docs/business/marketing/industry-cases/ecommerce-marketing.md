## Roadmap Overview

- **MVP (2025)**: Core segmentation, manual sale events, and basic recovery sequences.
- **Growth (2026)**: Behavioral personalization, automated omnichannel sync, and enhanced A/B testing.
- **Enterprise (2027)**: AI-powered pricing, predictive churn modeling, and IoT integration.

---

## Core Objectives

- **AOV Growth**: Increasing basket size through contextual cross-sells.
- **Retention**: Reducing churn via hyper-personalized re-engagement.
- **Efficiency**: Automating the marketing funnel to lower Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
