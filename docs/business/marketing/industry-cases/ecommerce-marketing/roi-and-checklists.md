## ROI Framework

### Core Growth Metrics

- **AOV (Average Order Value)**: Revenue per transaction.
- **CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)**: Total revenue over the customer's lifespan.
- **Repeat Purchase Rate**: Percentage of users who buy more than once.

### Calculation Logic

```markdown
ROI = (Revenue - Investment) / Investment × 100

CLV = (AOV) × (Frequency) × (Lifespan) × (Margin)
```

## Implementation Checklist

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

- [ ] Connect e-commerce store to PenguinMails.
- [ ] Set up abandoned cart triggers.
- [ ] Optimize product detail pages (PDP) for SEO.

### Phase 2: Campaign Development (Weeks 5-8)

- [ ] Launch 72-hour sale event.
- [ ] Deploy multi-tier loyalty program.
- [ ] Implement behavioral email sequences.

### Phase 3: Scaling (Weeks 9-12)

- [ ] Scale successful ad spend categories.
- [ ] Pilot AI-driven personalized recommendations.
- [ ] Transition to automated omnichannel reporting.
