## Sale Event Strategy

Strategic framework for 72-hour high-impact retail events.

### Campaign Phases

1. **Pre-Event (T-5 Days)**: Hype building via email teasers and social sneak peeks.
2. **Event Phase (72 Hours)**: Daily deals, countdown timers, and scarcity messaging.
3. **Post-Event (T+3 Days)**: Thank you emails, final abandoned cart recovery, and survey requests.

### Success Metrics

- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of event visitors who purchase.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Revenue per transaction during the event.
- **ROAS**: Return on ad spend for promotional traffic.

## Lifecycle Email Sequences

Standard automated triggers for e-commerce stores.

### 1. New Customer Welcome

- **Day 0**: Welcome + discount code.
- **Day 3**: Brand story + top categories.
- **Day 7**: Social proof + first review request.

### 2. Abandoned Cart Recovery

- **T+1 Hour**: Reminder of left items.
- **T+24 Hours**: Limited time offer/discount.
- **T+48 Hours**: Final scarcity-driven reminder.

### 3. Customer Win-back

- **T+30 Days (No purchase)**: "New arrivals" showcase.
- **T+90 Days**: Aggressive discount/re-activation offer.
