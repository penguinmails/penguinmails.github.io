## Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Awareness Stage

- **Customer Needs**: Discovery, brand recognition, social proof.
- **Marketing Strategies**:
  - **SEO**: Product page optimization for organic visibility.
  - **Social Media**: Visual showrooms on Instagram/TikTok.
  - **Influencers**: Partnership content and product reviews.

### Consideration Stage

- **Customer Needs**: Comparison, detailed specs, trust signals.
- **Marketing Strategies**:
  - **Email**: Product comparison guides.
  - **Retargeting**: Dynamic ads based on viewed products.
  - **Social Proof**: Highlighting customer testimonials and ratings.

### Purchase Stage

- **Customer Needs**: Streamlined checkout, secure payments, clear shipping info.
- **Marketing Strategies**:
  - **CRO**: Removing friction from the cart.
  - **Urgency**: Limited-time offers and "low stock" indicators.

## Customer Retention Strategy

### Post-Purchase Engagement

- **Goal**: Turn buyers into fans through fulfillment clarity and education.
- **Tactics**: Shipping updates, "unbox" guides, and point-based loyalty invitations.

### Re-engagement Campaigns

- **Goal**: Win back lapsed customers and drive repeat purchases.
- **Tactics**:
  - **Behavioral Email**: "We miss you" offers based on purchase history.
  - **SMS Alerts**: Time-sensitive sale notifications.
  - **Personalized Recommendations**: Showcasing new arrivals in preferred categories.
