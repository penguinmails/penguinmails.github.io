# Churn Prediction Model

### ML-Based Churn Risk Modeling Framework

#### Core Predictive Features Engineering

```yaml
Churn Prediction Features:
  Historical Health Score Trends:
    - Health score volatility and trend direction
    - Recovery patterns and intervention responsiveness
  Usage Pattern Analysis:
    - Feature adoption rates and product usage depth
    - User behavior patterns and behavioral drift
  Support Ticket Frequency:
    - Ticket volume trends and resolution time
    - Escalation patterns and severity level changes
  Stakeholder Engagement:
    - Executive sponsor engagement quality
    - Relationship mapping and influence analysis
  Payment Behavior:
    - Payment consistency and delayed invoice patterns
    - Contract renewal behavior signals
```

#### Risk Scoring & Intervention Protocol

### **High Risk (80-100% Probability)**

*Immediate Intervention Required*

- **Actions**: Executive escalation, custom retention plan, emergency resource allocation.
- **Resource Allocation**: Dedicated CSM assignment, technical support priority access.
- **Targets**: >15 point health score improvement within 30 days.

### **Medium Risk (50-79% Probability)**

*Proactive Outreach*

- **Actions**: Success plan review, additional training, stakeholder relationship enhancement.
- **Targets**: Value realization measurement and achieving >80% stakeholder response rate.

### **Low Risk (0-49% Probability)**

*Standard Monitoring*

- **Actions**: Standard check-in schedule, expansion opportunity identification.
- **Targets**: Case study development and ROI maximization strategies.

#### Model Performance & Accuracy

- **Precision**: Target >85% for high-risk predictions.
- **Recall Rate**: Target >80% for actual churn identification.
- **Interpretability**: SHAP values for individual prediction interpretation and business logic validation.
