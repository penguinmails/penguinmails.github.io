# Expansion Forecasting Model

### Revenue Opportunity Prediction Framework

#### Opportunity Scoring & Qualification

```yaml
Expansion Opportunity Scoring:
  Usage Capacity:
    - Feature utilization rates and user adoption levels
    - Capacity indicators and expansion readiness signals
  Growth Trajectory:
    - Historical growth patterns and seasonal adjustment
    - Investment and scaling signal identification
  Satisfaction Levels:
    - Executive sponsor satisfaction and product advocacy
    - Relationship health and trust foundation
  Budget Signals:
    - Budget cycle timing and investment approval processes
    - Financial health and payment behavior analysis
```

#### Timeline Predictions

### **30-Day Window: Immediate**

- **Signals**: Budget approval in progress, champion identification, urgent business need.
- **Actions**: procurement stage assessment, value proposition customization.

### **90-Day Window: Strategic**

- **Signals**: Quarterly planning cycle alignment, legacy system replacement timelines.
- **Actions**: Strategic initiative prioritization, technology roadmap alignment.

### **12-Month Window: Long-term**

- **Signals**: Annual strategic planning, market expansion initiatives, geographic growth.
- **Actions**: Technology integration and ecosystem expansion project planning.

#### Revenue Impact Modeling

- **Upsell Probability**: Confidence scoring based on feature adoption correlation.
- **Cross-sell Sizing**: Customer journey mapping and trigger identification.
- **Time-to-Close**: Sales cycle modeling and budget cycle alignment optimization.
- **Forecast Accuracy**: Target >90% accuracy for dollar impact predictions.
