# Performance Case Studies

## SaaS Scale-Up: 50K → 150K Monthly Emails

### **Challenge**

Maintaining performance and sales efficiency while tripling monthly email volume.

### **Solution**

- **Infrastructure**: Upgraded to 5 dedicated IPs with optimized rotation.
- **Content**: Implemented dynamic personalization and industry-specific templates.
- **Process**: Automated list management and real-time performance dashboards.

### **Results**

- **Open Rate**: 32% (Maintained vs 25% industry average).
- **Cost per Meeting**: Reduced by 40%.
- **Sales Efficiency**: 25% reduction in time-to-meeting.

## Agency Recovery: Deliverability Restoration

### **Challenge**

Domain reputation damage leading to 60% deliverability and high spam complaints.

### **Solution**

- **Emergency**: Implemented DMARC quarantine and paused damaged lists.
- **Recovery**: New IP allocation with 6-week gradual warming protocol.
- **Hygiene**: Stricter list verification and enhanced bounce handling.

### **Results**

- **Deliverability**: Recovered to 94% within 8 weeks.
- **Spam Complaints**: Reduced from 0.8% to 0.05%.
- **Restoration**: Full campaign performance restored in 12 weeks.

---

## Enterprise: Multi-Provider Optimization

### **Challenge**

Inconsistent performance across multiple ESPs and lack of centralized analytics.

### **Solution**

- **Consolidation**: Standardized on primary ESP with secondary for A/B testing.
- **Analytics**: Unified performance dashboard with cross-provider attribution.
- **Strategy**: Provider-specific content optimization based on performance data.

### **Results**

- **Overall Deliverability**: Improved to 96%.
- **Cost Efficiency**: 30% reduction in per-email costs.
- **Scalability**: Reached 500K+ emails/month with 50% management overhead reduction.
