# What is PenguinMails?

PenguinMails is an enterprise-grade cold email infrastructure platform designed to solve the deliverability challenges of professional sales and marketing teams. By automating the technical foundation of email—servers, DNS, warm-up, and compliance—we allow you to focus on your message while we ensure it actually reaches your prospect's inbox.
